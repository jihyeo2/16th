I know it's been more than half an year since my last post, but but buttttt--to break the cycle and prevent the unfortunate birth of my 17th blog--I am back! 

And you know what? I'm now living in a different state, sleeping on my friend's couch. It's like I'm back in 2024. (Yes, this has happened before. I know, I have great friends. Genuinely.) 

I've actually started writing this update several times over the past few weeks, but I kept losing steam after a few words, which is why it never came together.

But today, I just felt so grateful for the people around me: my friends, my family, and a lot of people I've only just gotten to know. They are all so kind and willing to help, and I honestly don't know how they can be like that. 

May the situation I'm in isn't not the best, but the people around me are helping me hold myself together. And I thank God for sending them to me.

I really don't know where my path will lead, but I've decided: I'll try my best, and accept whatever the result is. (Think I said it before in my other blog.)

So as a tribute to everyone who helped me at any point, I wanted to write this blog to thank them. 

I guess hardships really make you reflect on how much there is to be thankful for, ironically. And I hope someday I can be that helping hand for someone else.
